Ready-to-copy columns for 25 fields. Open a field, then either:

- **Paste its research question** into the setup dialog and click **Suggest columns**, or
- **Copy the columns you want.** In **Manage Columns → New column**, paste the Field into **Name** and the Instructions into **Instructions**, and choose the **Type** shown.

Edit the wording to match your papers. For how to write your own columns, see [Tips for creating better extraction fields](tips-for-better-extraction-fields).

## Health & clinical research

<Expandable title="1. Clinical medicine: intervention trials" default-open="false" title-type="h3">
  Example topic: effect of early mobilisation on ICU length of stay.

  Research question to try: _Does early mobilisation shorten ICU length of stay in mechanically ventilated adults?_

  | Field              | Type                                                                                                                           | Instructions                                                                                                                                                                           |
  | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | Study design       | Pick from a list: RCT, Cluster RCT, Non-randomised trial, Prospective cohort, Retrospective cohort, Case-control, Before-after | Classify the design as the authors label it. Pick the single best fit.                                                                                                                 |
  | Analysed sample    | Free text                                                                                                                      | Number of participants in the primary analysis, not screened or randomised. Total first, per-arm in parentheses if reported. Format: "294 (148/146)".                                  |
  | Population         | Free text                                                                                                                      | Who was studied: condition, mean or median age with its spread, and % female, exactly as printed. Format: "Mechanically ventilated ICU adults; mean age 62.4 (SD 14.1); 41% female".   |
  | Intervention       | Free text                                                                                                                      | What the intervention group received: what was done, how often, for how long, and by whom. Format: "Progressive mobilisation, twice daily, from ICU day 1, physiotherapist-delivered". |
  | Comparator         | Free text                                                                                                                      | What the control group received, in the authors' words. If usual care, say what usual care included if described.                                                                      |
  | Primary outcome    | Free text                                                                                                                      | The outcome the authors designate as primary, and how it was measured. Do not list secondary outcomes here.                                                                            |
  | Primary result     | Free text                                                                                                                      | The effect on the primary outcome with its 95% CI and p-value exactly as printed, naming the metric. Format: "MD −1.9 days (95% CI −3.1 to −0.7), p=0.002".                            |
  | Follow-up          | Free text                                                                                                                      | Longest timepoint at which outcomes were measured, in the paper's unit. Format: "90 days".                                                                                             |
  | Adverse events     | Free text                                                                                                                      | Serious adverse events with denominators, per arm, exactly as printed. Do not convert counts to percentages. If none reported, answer "Not reported".                                  |
  | Author limitations | Free text                                                                                                                      | Limitations the authors state themselves, one short bullet each. Do not add your own.                                                                                                  |
</Expandable>
