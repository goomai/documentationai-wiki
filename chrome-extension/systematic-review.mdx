title: Get PDFs in Systematic Review with the Chrome Extension
---
## Overview

Full text screening needs a PDF for every paper. When papers move into full text screening, Paperguide fetches open-access PDFs automatically and reuses PDFs already in your Reference Manager. Papers it can't get go to the **PDF Missing** tab and wait there until a PDF is attached.

Use the Paperguide Chrome extension to find the rest in bulk, attach PDFs you already have, or ask an AI assistant to find them through MCP.

**Who can add PDFs:** members with **Full Access** or **Review** access. Members with **View** access can only download the list of missing papers. See [Members & Roles](/systematic-review/members-roles).

## Before you start

- [Install the Chrome extension](/chrome-extension) and log in with your Paperguide account.
- If your university or library gives you access to paywalled journals, [set up library access](/chrome-extension/library-access) first. The extension then tries your library for every paper in the search.

## Get missing PDFs for the whole review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open full text screening" icon="file-search" title-type="p">
    Open your review and go to **Full Text Screening**. The **PDF Missing** tab lists every paper that still needs a PDF.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Get missing PDFs and choose an option" icon="download" title-type="p">
    **Get missing PDFs** is in the toolbar on every tab while any paper still lacks a PDF. Choose:

    - **Find PDFs online**: opens the **Find PDFs** dialog, where the Chrome extension finds and attaches the PDFs. See [Use the Find PDFs dialog](#use-the-find-pdfs-dialog) below.
    - **Attach PDFs**: upload PDFs you already have. See [Upload PDFs you already have](#upload-pdfs-you-already-have).
    - **Add with MCP**: opens a ready-made prompt in Claude or ChatGPT, or copies it for Gemini. Your AI assistant finds the missing PDFs and attaches them.


  </Step>
</Steps>

### Use the Find PDFs dialog

PDFs found here are added to your review. In an **AI Review**, screening starts on its own for each paper that gets a PDF. In a **Dual Review**, the paper enters both reviewers' queues.

## Upload PDFs you already have

Click **Get missing PDFs → Attach PDFs** to open **Bulk upload PDFs**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop your PDFs" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Drop up to 200 PDFs, or click **Browse**. Paperguide matches each file to its paper by the DOI printed in the PDF.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the matches" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
    Files are grouped by how well they matched:

    - **Matched**: the DOI matched a paper.
    - **Check match**: a likely match. Confirm it's the right paper.
    - **Needs a paper**: no match found. Choose the paper yourself from the missing list.
    - **Already has a PDF**: that paper doesn't need this file.


  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach" icon="paperclip" title-type="p">
    Tick the files you want and click **Attach**. Papers leave **PDF Missing** as each one finishes processing. In an AI Review, each attached paper is screened by AI and uses credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Download the list of missing papers

Don't have the PDFs yet? In **Bulk upload PDFs**, download the missing papers as **RIS** or **CSV**. Import the RIS file into Zotero or EndNote and use its find full text feature, then upload the PDFs it finds. Members with View access see this as **Download list** in the toolbar.

## Get the PDF for one paper

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add PDF on the paper" icon="paperclip" title-type="p">
    In the **PDF Missing** tab, click **Add PDF** on the paper.


  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an option" icon="mouse-pointer-click" title-type="p">
    - **Find PDF online**: Paperguide finds the PDF and attaches it automatically.
    - **Locate PDF**: opens the paper's page. Open the PDF there, and Paperguide attaches it.
    - **Search Google for PDF**: searches Google for the paper's PDF in a new tab.
    - **Attach PDF**: upload a PDF from your computer.
    - **Add with MCP**: your AI assistant finds the PDF and attaches it.

    The first three options use the Chrome extension.


  </Step>
</Steps>

### Why a paper is still missing its PDF

Each paper in **PDF Missing** shows the reason. The most common ones:

| Message                                                       | What to do                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| This paper isn't open access.                                 | [Set up library access](/chrome-extension/library-access), or upload a PDF you have. |
| Couldn't fetch the PDF automatically.                         | Try **Find PDF online** or **Locate PDF**, or upload it.                             |
| The publisher blocked automatic download.                     | Use **Locate PDF** to open the page yourself, or upload it.                          |
| The fetched file wasn't a valid PDF.                          | Upload the correct file.                                                             |
| The PDF attached belongs to a different paper in this review. | Upload the right PDF for this paper.                                                 |
| The PDF is over the 100 MB limit.                             | Upload a smaller copy.                                                               |

## What happens to papers without a PDF

You don't have to find every PDF before you start screening. A paper without a PDF is held out of screening and counted as **not retrieved** in your PRISMA diagram. You can add its PDF at any time, even after the stage is verified, and it is then screened like the rest.

Every paper you leave without a PDF stays in your review's reported totals as not retrieved, so try to resolve them before you write up. See [AI Review full text screening](/systematic-review/ai-review/full-text-screening) and [Dual Review full text screening](/systematic-review/dual-review/full-text-screening).
