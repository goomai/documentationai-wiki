## Copy a citation

Find a reference in your library and copy its citation without switching to Paperguide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the extension" icon="chrome" title-type="p">
    Click the Paperguide icon in your browser toolbar, from any tab, such as the document or email you're writing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search your library" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Use the search bar to find the reference.

    <Image uid="f938d46e-a0de-4323-ba11-24aa56e5dd8b" src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1763990982057-scewp9uztw-chrome-extension-citation-management.pn" width="518" height="134" alt="Searching the library in the Paperguide extension" style="width: 518px; height: auto; cursor: pointer;" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy and paste" icon="copy" title-type="p">
    Click to copy the formatted citation, then paste it into your document, email, slides or notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask AI about the page you're reading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the extension on the page" icon="globe" title-type="p">
    Go to the paper or web page, and click the Paperguide icon in your browser toolbar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Ask AI" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    Click **Ask AI** and start chatting with the page that's open.


  </Step>
</Steps>

Ask for a summary, a definition, an explanation of the methods, a comparison with other work, or anything else about what you're reading.
