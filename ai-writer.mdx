AI Writer is an editor for research writing. Describe your document to get a cited draft or an outline, or write it yourself with AI Chat and AI tools beside you. Every citation links to its paper and the sentences behind it.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Free plan: generated documents up to 3,000 words. Exporting, copying text out of the editor and viewing the References list need a paid plan. AI features use AI credits.
</Callout>

## Start a document

Go to **AI Writer** in the left menu. You can start in three ways:

| Option                                        | What happens                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Generate Document** or **Generate Outline** | Describe your document and AI writes a cited draft or an outline. See [Create a document](/ai-writer/create-document). |
| **Open blank document**                       | An empty document with every AI tool available.                                                                        |
| **Import Word Document**                      | Upload a .docx file. Its first heading becomes the document title.                                                     |

## The workspace

Your document opens in the editor, with **AI Chat** on the right. Click **AI Chat** in the top bar to show or hide it.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1795671586944-g3ur0ngskxi-05-editor-and-chat.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1456" height="827" alt="A cited document in the editor with AI Chat open beside it" />

- **Toolbar:** **Cite**, text styles, image, table, inline equation and math block, plus the **Smart Continue** switch.
- **Top bar:** **AI Chat**, **Share**, **Review** (grammar and plagiarism), **Comments**, and **More** (Settings, My References, Versions, Export).
- **Sidebar:** your documents and folders. Click ☰ next to the breadcrumb to open it.

## Citations

### Add a citation

Type **@** or click **Cite** in the toolbar. Search **My References** (your Reference Manager) or **Research Database**, then click **Cite** on a paper.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1795671625430-lrjn2jndlg-12-cite-popover.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1470" height="779" alt="The Cite popover with My References and Research Database tabs" />

### Check a citation

- **Hover** a citation to see the paper: journal, year, citation count and journal quality. Add it to **My References** or **Open link**.
- **Click** a citation to open the paper. **Supporting quotes** show the exact sentences that back the claim, highlighted in the abstract or full text.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1795671594910-noirbgv4aqs-07-supporting-quotes.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1456" height="827" alt="A cited paper opened from the document, with its supporting quote highlighted in the abstract" />

Supporting quotes are shown for text written by AI.

### Citation style and references

The **References** list at the end of the document updates as you cite. Click **Copy all citations** to copy it. To change the style, go to **More → Settings → Citation Style**. Every citation and the list update together.
