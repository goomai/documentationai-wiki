## How AI credits work

Paperguide agents and AI features consume credits whenever they run tasks such as literature reviews, deep research reports, or data extraction. Each request uses a certain number of credits based on how much AI processing is required.

Credits are tied to your Paperguide plan and refresh every month on your billing date.

### Features that consume AI credits

The following features use AI credits each time they run:

- Systematic Review
- Deep Research Report
- Extract Data
- Research Agent Chats (Research agent, Literature Review Agent, AI Chats in Writer and Reference Manager)
- AI Writer - Generate Document
- AI Writer – AI Edit

## Estimating AI credit usage

Use the examples below to understand what you typically get each month from your AI credits on each plan.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The numbers below are approximations. Actual usage may vary depending on task complexity, content length, references included and the specific AI features you use.
</Callout>

### Systematic review

A systematic review starting with **1,000 papers screened:**

With **12,500 credits/month (Plus)**, you can run **up to 2** systematic reviews of this scale each month.

With **50,000 credits/month (Pro)**, you can run **up to 10** systematic reviews of this scale each month.

With **150,000 credits/month (Max)**, you can run **up to 30** systematic reviews of this scale each month.

### Systematic review

A systematic review starting with **5,000 papers screened:**

With **12,500 credits/month (Plis)**, a full 5,000-paper systematic review is not feasible.

With **50,000 credits/month (Pro)**, you can run **up to 2** systematic reviews of this scale each month.

With **150,000 credits/month (Max)**, you can run **up to 6-7** systematic reviews of this scale each month.

### Literature review

With **12,500 credits/month (Plus)**, you can get around **15-17 literature reviews** spread across your projects each month.

With **50,000 credits/month (Pro)**, you can get around **60-70 literature reviews** for larger or more frequent research workflows.

With **150,000 credits/month (Max)**, you can get around **180-200 literature reviews** for larger or more frequent research workflows.
