## How AI credits work

Paperguide's AI features use credits when they run. How many depends on how much work the AI does: the number of papers, how long they are, and how much it writes.

Credits come with your plan and refresh every month on your billing date. To see what you have left, go to **Settings → Usage**.

| Plan     | Credits per month |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Free     | 1,500             |
| Plus     | 12,500            |
| Pro      | 50,000            |
| Evidence | 100,000           |

Credits are per seat. An organization shares one pool: the plan's credits multiplied by its number of seats.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Evidence plans bought before 20 November 2026 keep 150,000 credits per month.
</Callout>

## What uses credits

- **Systematic Review**: screening, full-text review, data extraction, and the report.
- **Extract Data**: each time columns are extracted.
- **Literature Review**
- **Research Agent**: chat answers, based on how much research each answer needs.
- **AI Writer**: generates documents; AI Chat, Smart Continue, Write with AI, and AI Edit; usage depends on how much you use them.
- **MCP server**: search papers and read full texts with your AI assistant. Browsing your library and reading or writing documents are free. See [MCP credits](/mcp/credits).

These don't use credits:

- AI Search
- Chat with PDF
- The plagiarism check, which has its own monthly word limit

## What your credits cover

Here's roughly what each plan's monthly credits cover if you use them on one feature:

| Feature                              | Plus            | Pro            | Evidence        |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Extract Data, papers with 10 columns | 550–1,000       | 2,200–4,200    | 4,400–8,300     |
| Systematic Review, 1,000 papers      | About 2 reviews | 8–10 reviews   | 15–20 reviews   |
| Systematic Review, 5,000 papers      | -               | 1–2 reviews    | 3–4 reviews     |
| Literature Review                    | 20–30 reviews   | 80–140 reviews | 170–280 reviews |

Research Agent, AI Writer and the other AI features use credits based on how much you use them.

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false" color="#f59e0b">
  These numbers are based on average usage across Paperguide. Your actual usage depends on your context and references, and on the length and complexity of your PDFs.
</Callout>

## Need more credits?

On a paid plan, you can buy credit packs of 5,000, 10,000, 20,000, 40,000 or 100,000 credits from **Settings → Billing → Buy credits**. Add-on credits are valid for 12 months and can be used while your plan is active.

You can also upgrade your plan for more monthly credits.
