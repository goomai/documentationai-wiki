## What is Paperguide?

Paperguide is an all-in-one AI research platform that brings search, analysis, literature review, systematic review, reference management, and document writing into a single workspace. Whether you are exploring a new topic, running a formal systematic review, or drafting a research paper, Paperguide's AI agents handle the repetitive parts so you can focus on insight.

## How it works

Paperguide organizes your work into organizations and workspaces. Each workspace has its own Reference Manager, AI Writer, Systematic Review, Extract Data, and Notebooks.

AI agents including Research Agent, AI Search, Literature Review, Deep Research, and Chat with PDF are available from the sidebar and share context with your workspace library. Learn more in [Organizations & Workspaces](/organizations-workspaces).
