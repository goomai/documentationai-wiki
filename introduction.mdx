## What is Paperguide?

Paperguide is an all-in-one AI research platform that brings search, analysis, literature review, systematic review, reference management, and document writing into a single workspace. Whether you are exploring a new topic, running a formal systematic review, or drafting a research paper, Paperguide's AI agents handle the repetitive parts so you can focus on insight.

## How it works

Paperguide organizes your work into workspaces. Each workspace has its own Reference Manager, AI Writer, Systematic Review, Extract Data, and Notebooks. Use **Citation Graph** in the sidebar to explore how papers connect.

To use an agent, click **New Chat** in the sidebar (or use the box on the **Dashboard**) and pick a tab: **Research Agent**, **Literature Review**, **Deep Research Report**, or **Search**. **Chat with PDF**, **AI Search**, and **Free Tools** are under **More tools**. Agents can search 200M+ papers and your own library. To look for open questions in a field, try the **Research gap analysis** task on the Research Agent tab.

Learn more in [Dashboard and Navigation](/dashboard-and-navigation) and [Organizations & Workspaces](/organizations-workspaces).
