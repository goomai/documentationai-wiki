## Overview

If you already use Zotero or Mendeley, you can connect your account and import your library directly. You don't need to export any files. Paperguide brings over:

- **References**, with their details (title, authors, year, journal, DOI and abstract)
- **PDFs**, when **Import PDFs** is on
- **Structure**: each Zotero collection or Mendeley folder you select becomes a folder in Paperguide

Imports run in the background, and you can import again later to bring over only the papers you've added since.

## Import from Zotero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import from Zotero" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    In Reference Manager, click **Add Papers** and choose **Import from Zotero**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your Zotero account" icon="link" title-type="p">
    The first time, click **Connect Zotero**. On the zotero.org permission page, click **Accept Defaults**. You don't need to change anything there. You'll return to Paperguide with your Zotero library loaded.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to import" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
    Your collections are listed on the left. Tick a whole collection, or open one and tick only the papers you need.

    A ticked collection becomes a folder in Paperguide and its papers are filed into it. Papers you tick on their own are added to your library without a folder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keep Import PDFs on" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    With **Import PDFs** on, PDFs come over with their references.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the import" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Click **Import references**. If you have more than one workspace, choose where the references should go and click **Import here**.

    The import runs in the background, so you can keep working. A progress card shows **Importing Papers** while it runs and **Import Completed** when it's done.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find your papers" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Each collection you imported now appears as a folder in the sidebar, with its papers and PDFs inside.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Paperguide can import PDFs that are synced to your Zotero storage. PDFs stored only on your computer aren't imported. In the import results these are marked "PDF not synced to Zotero, only on your computer".
</Callout>

The Zotero dialog also has **Refresh**, to reload your library after changes in Zotero, and **Disconnect**, to unlink your Zotero account.

## Import from Mendeley

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import from Mendeley" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    In Reference Manager, click **Add Papers** and choose **Import from Mendeley**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your Mendeley account" icon="link" title-type="p">
    The first time, click **Connect Mendeley**. A popup opens where you sign in to Mendeley and allow access. After you approve, your Mendeley library appears in Paperguide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to import" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
    Tick whole folders, or open a folder and tick individual references.

    A ticked folder is recreated in Paperguide and its references are filed into it. References you tick on their own are added to your library without a folder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Keep **Import PDFs** on so your files come across too, then click **Import references**. If you have more than one workspace, choose where the references should go and click **Import here**.

    The import runs in the background while you keep working.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find your papers" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    When it's finished, your folders appear in the sidebar, laid out the way you had them in Mendeley.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Importing again

If you keep adding papers in Zotero or Mendeley, run the import again. Papers you imported before are skipped, and existing folders are reused, so you only get what's new. Skipped Zotero papers are marked "Already imported from Zotero earlier" in the import results.

To see what happened to each paper, click **Show results** on the progress card.

## Stopping an import

To stop an import that's running, click the **✕** next to it on the progress card, then click **Stop import**. References already added stay in your library. To finish later, import the same collection or folder again.

## Other ways to import

Also using EndNote or another tool? Export your library as a BibTeX (`.bib`) or RIS (`.ris`) file, then click **Add Papers** and choose **BibTeX / RIS**. BibTeX and RIS files carry reference details only, not PDFs. To add PDFs afterwards, see [Get PDFs with the Chrome Extension](/reference-manager/get-pdfs-with-extension).
