## Overview

Your library doesn't just store papers. Save a paper once, and you can use it everywhere in Paperguide.

## Research Agent and Literature Review

By default, your questions and literature reviews use **all sources**: research papers, PubMed, arXiv and your Reference Manager. You can narrow this down to just the papers you've saved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sources" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    Below the question box, click the source button. It shows **All sources** by default. The **Sources** panel lists **Research papers**, **PubMed**, **arXiv** and **Reference manager**, each with a switch.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1793813227130-1no3rxkc85d-research-agent-sources.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1600" height="1008" alt="The Sources panel in Research Agent with all four sources on" caption="All four sources are on by default. Use Choose papers or folders to narrow your library." />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose papers or folders" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    With **Reference manager** on, click **Choose papers or folders** and pick the papers or folders to use. To answer only from your library, switch the other sources off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your question" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    Type your question and send it. The answer, or the literature report, uses the sources you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also add papers to a question with **+ → Add from reference manager**.

See [Research Agent](/research-agent) and [Literature Review Agent](/literature-review-agent).

## Systematic Review

Bring papers from your library into a systematic review to screen them. On the review's **Papers** page, click **Add Papers**, choose **Reference manager**, and pick the folder or papers to add.

See [Systematic Review](/systematic-review/overview).

## Extract Data

Pull the key data from your papers into a table. Click **Select References**, choose the folder or papers from your library, and Paperguide extracts the data into columns you can add to, such as sample size.

You can select up to 10 papers on the free plan and up to 100 on paid plans. Papers whose PDFs aren't indexed yet are indexed first.

See [Extract Data](/extract-data/overview).

## AI Writer

Write with citations that come straight from your library.

- **Cite from your library**: in a document, click **Cite**, or type **@**. Search on the **My References** tab and add the citation. The **Research Database** tab searches public papers instead.
- **Let the AI use your library**: in the document's settings, turn on **Your Reference Manager** and click **Choose folders** to pick which folders it draws from.

See [AI Writer](/ai-writer/overview).

## Your AI assistant (MCP)

Connect Paperguide to an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT through MCP. Your assistant can then find papers in your library, search inside your PDFs, add papers, attach PDFs and organise folders, all from your chat.

See [MCP: Reference Manager](/mcp/reference-manager) and [Connect](/mcp/connect-and-manage).

## Inside Reference Manager

You can also work with papers without leaving your library:

- **AI Chat** in the toolbar lets you chat with the papers in the current view, or with the papers you've selected.
- The **Chat** tab in a paper's detail panel lets you chat with that one paper.
- **Cite** copies a paper's full citation or in-text citation.
- **Export** downloads papers as **BibTeX (.bib)** or **RIS (.ris)** to use in other tools.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Some tools can only use papers that have a PDF attached, or in some cases an abstract. When you pick papers, a tip tells you if one can't be selected and why. See [Get PDFs with Extension](/chrome-extension/reference-manager).
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  On Pro and higher plans, AI features also read the tables, figures and scanned pages in your PDFs, and extract data from them.
</Callout>
