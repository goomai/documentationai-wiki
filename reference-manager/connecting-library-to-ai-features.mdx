## Overview

Your library doesn't just store papers. Save a paper once, and you can use it everywhere in Paperguide.

## Research Agent and Literature Review

Ask questions or generate a literature review from the papers you've saved, instead of searching public papers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your library as the source" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    Below the question box, click the source button (it shows **Public Research Papers** by default) and choose **Reference Manager**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the papers" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Choose the folder or papers to use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your question" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    Type your question and send it. The answer, or the literature report, is based on your chosen papers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Research Agent](/research-agent) and [Literature Review Agent](/literature-review-agent).

## Systematic Review

Bring papers from your library into a systematic review to screen them. On the review's **Papers** page, click **Add Papers**, choose **Reference manager**, and pick the folder or papers to add.

See [Systematic Review](/systematic-review/overview).

## Extract Data

Pull the key data from your papers into a table. Click **Select References**, choose the folder or papers from your library, and Paperguide extracts the data into columns you can add to, such as sample size.

See [Extract Data](/extract-data/overview).

## AI Writer

Write with citations that come straight from your library. In a document, click **Cite**, search for the paper and add the citation.

See [AI Writer](/ai-writer/overview).

## Inside Reference Manager

You can also work with papers without leaving your library:

- **AI Chat** in the toolbar lets you chat with the papers in the current view, or with the papers you've selected.
- The **Chat** tab in a paper's detail panel lets you chat with that one paper.
- **Cite** copies a paper's full citation or in-text citation.
- **Export** downloads papers as **BibTeX (.bib)** or **RIS (.ris)** to use in other tools.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Some tools can only use papers that have a PDF attached, or in some cases an abstract. When you pick papers, a tip tells you if one can't be selected and why. See [Get PDFs with the Chrome Extension](/reference-manager/get-pdfs-with-extension).
</Callout>
