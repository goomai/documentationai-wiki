## Overview

You can read, mark up and take notes on a paper without leaving your library. Your highlights and notes stay attached to the paper, in every folder it's in.

## Open a paper to read

Select a paper in your library to open its detail panel, then click **View PDF**. You can also open the paper's **Notes** tab and click **View PDF and Annotate**.

## Mark up the PDF

The toolbar at the top of the reader has these tools:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Highlight** | Select text to highlight it |
| **Underline** | Select text to underline it |
| **Draw** | Drag a box around an area, such as a figure or table, to capture the whole area |
| Colour picker | Choose the colour for your highlights, for example yellow for key findings and blue for methods |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a tool" icon="highlighter" title-type="p">
    Click **Highlight**, **Underline** or **Draw**, and choose a colour.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mark the text or area" icon="mouse-pointer" title-type="p">
    Select a passage of text, or with **Draw**, drag a box around a figure or table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a note" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    Write a note on the highlight to record why it matters while the thought is fresh.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review your notes in the Notes tab

Back in your library, select the paper and open the **Notes** tab in its detail panel. It lists every highlight and note on the paper, each with the page it came from, so you can review them at a glance.

To edit or delete a note, open its **Note options** menu and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

### Quick notes

For thoughts about the paper as a whole, such as where it fits in your thesis, type in the **Add quick note...** box at the top of the Notes tab. Quick notes aren't tied to a page.

## Notes in shared folders

When you share a folder, collaborators with **Can Add Notes** or **Can Edit** access can highlight and write notes on its papers too. Their notes appear alongside yours, with their names, so the discussion happens right on the page. See [Share Library & Workspace](/reference-manager/share-library-workspace).

## Notes stay with the paper

A paper's notes and highlights belong to the paper, not to a folder. If the paper is in several folders, you see the same notes in each one.
