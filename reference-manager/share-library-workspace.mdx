## Overview

Instead of emailing PDFs back and forth, share your papers so everyone works from the same library. There are two ways to share:

- [**Share folders**](#share-folders) with specific people by email. Use this for a supervisor, co-authors or anyone outside your team.
- [**Share a workspace**](#share-workspace) in your organization. Everyone added to the workspace works from the same library, with no folder invites needed.

## Share folders

### Share a folder

You can share a folder you created. In a team workspace, workspace owners and editors can share its folders too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder's options" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    In the sidebar, open the folder's **Folder options** menu and choose **Share Folder**. If the folder is already shared, the option is called **Manage Access**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter an email address" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Type your colleague's email address in **Enter email to invite**.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1793813235279-xhgxp87ldvm-share-folder-dialog.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1200" height="640" alt="The Share Folder dialog" caption="Enter an email, choose a permission, and click Invite." />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a permission" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    Pick what they can do (see the table below).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invite" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Click **Invite**. Your colleague gets an email invitation and a notification in Paperguide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Permissions

| Permission        | What they can do                                                                                                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Can View**      | Read the folder's papers                                                                                           |
| **Can Add Notes** | Read the papers, plus highlight and write notes on them                                                            |
| **Can Edit**      | Add and change papers and notes, and create subfolders. They can't share the folder, or rename, move or delete it. |

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  For a supervisor, **Can Add Notes** is often just right: they can comment on your papers without changing the folder.
</Callout>

### Accept an invitation

The invitation opens a page where your colleague clicks **Accept** or **Decline**. Until they accept, they show as **Pending** in the share dialog. If they decline, they show as **Rejected**.

Once they accept, the folder appears under **Shared With Me** in their Reference Manager.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  When a folder is shared from an organization, the person who accepts is added to that organization as a guest. The folder appears under **Shared With Me** in that organization's Reference Manager, not in their personal library. To find it, they switch to that organization.
</Callout>

### Work together

When you share a folder, you both work from the same papers. Collaborators with **Can Add Notes** or **Can Edit** access can highlight passages and leave notes, and you'll see their notes next to yours. See [Notes & Annotation](/reference-manager/notes-annotation).

### Change or remove access

Open the folder's **Folder options** menu and choose **Manage Access**. Under **People with access** you can:

- Change someone's permission using the menu next to their name. This menu appears once they've accepted the invitation.
- Click **Remove access** (the trash icon) to stop sharing the folder with them, or to cancel a pending invitation.

If someone has shared a folder with you and you no longer need it, open its **Folder options** menu and choose **Leave Folder**.

## Share workspace

If your whole team uses Paperguide, sharing a team workspace is simpler than sharing folders one by one. Members see the team workspaces they've been added to. Organization owners and admins can access every workspace. What each member can do in a workspace depends on their workspace role.

You can also share a folder from a team workspace with people outside your organization, using **Share Folder** as described above. Whether you can depends on your organization's **External sharing** setting: **Anyone**, **Same domain only**, or **No external sharing**. People who already have access through the team workspace don't need an invite.

To set up a team workspace and manage roles, see [Organizations & Workspaces](/organizations-workspaces).
