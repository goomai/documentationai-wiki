## Overview

Instead of emailing PDFs back and forth, share a folder so everyone works from the same papers. There are two ways to share:

- **Share a folder** with specific people by email. Use this for a supervisor, co-authors or anyone outside your team.
- **Use a team workspace** to give everyone in your organization a shared library, with no invites needed.

## Share a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder's options" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    In the sidebar, open the folder's **Folder options** menu and choose **Share Folder**. If the folder is already shared, the option is called **Manage Access**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter an email address" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Type your colleague's email address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a permission" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    Pick what they can do (see the table below).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invite" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Click **Invite**. Your colleague gets an email invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Permissions

| Permission | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Can View** | Read the folder's papers |
| **Can Add Notes** | Read the papers, plus highlight and write notes on them |
| **Can Edit** | Full control of the folder |

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  For a supervisor, **Can Add Notes** is often just right: they can comment on your papers without changing the folder.
</Callout>

## Accept an invitation

The invitation email opens a page where your colleague clicks **Accept** or **Reject**. Until they accept, they show as **Pending** in the share dialog.

Once they accept, the folder appears in their own library under **Shared With Me**.

## Work together

When you share a folder, you both work from the same papers. Collaborators with **Can Add Notes** or **Can Edit** access can highlight passages and leave notes, and you'll see their notes next to yours. See [Notes & Annotation](/reference-manager/notes-annotation).

## Change or remove access

Open the folder's **Folder options** menu and choose **Manage Access**. Under **People with access** you can:

- Change someone's permission using the menu next to their name.
- Click **Remove access** to stop sharing the folder with them.

If someone has shared a folder with you and you no longer need it, open its **Folder options** menu and choose **Leave Folder**.

## Team workspaces

If your whole team uses Paperguide, a team workspace is simpler. Everyone in your organization gets a shared library without any invites. What each member can do depends on their workspace role.

You can still share a folder from a team workspace with people outside your organization, using **Share Folder** as described above.

To set up a team workspace and manage roles, see [Organizations & Workspaces](/organizations-workspaces).
