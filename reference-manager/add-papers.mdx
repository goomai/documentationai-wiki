## Overview

Every way of adding papers starts from the **Add Papers** button in Reference Manager. It opens a window with these options:

| Option | Use it when you have |
|---|---|
| **Upload PDF** | PDF files on your computer |
| **URL / DOI Import** | DOIs or links to papers |
| **Search Online** | A title, author, journal, publisher or topic, but no link |
| **BibTeX / RIS** | A `.bib` or `.ris` file, or citation text, from EndNote or another tool |
| **Import from Zotero** | A Zotero library |
| **Import from Mendeley** | A Mendeley library |
| **Add Manually** | A source with no online record |

Paperguide fills in each paper's details (title, authors, journal and so on) for you, so there's usually nothing to type.

## Choose where papers go

Before you import, pick the destination under **Save to workspace / folder**. Papers are added to that workspace and filed into the folder you choose.

Keep **Skip duplicates** on (it's on by default) so papers already in your library aren't added again. See [Handling duplicates](/reference-manager/duplicates).

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Zotero and Mendeley imports work a little differently: each collection or folder you tick becomes a folder in Paperguide. See [Import from Zotero & Mendeley](/reference-manager/import-zotero-mendeley).
</Callout>

## Upload PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Upload PDF" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **Upload PDF**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your files" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Drag your PDFs into the window, or click **browse files** to pick them. You can upload up to 30 PDFs at once.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click the upload button, which shows how many files you've added (for example **Upload 3 PDFs**). Paperguide reads each file and fills in the title, authors and journal. Each PDF becomes a reference with the file attached.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## URL / DOI Import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose URL / DOI Import" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **URL / DOI Import**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste your DOIs or links" icon="clipboard" title-type="p">
    Paste a DOI (for example `10.1038/s41586-020-2649-2`) or a link to a paper. To add several papers, paste one DOI or link per line.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click **Import Reference** (or **Import 3 References** for a list). Paperguide looks up each paper's details. When an open-access PDF is available, it's attached too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  DOIs and direct links to publisher pages give the most complete details. If Paperguide can't find details for a link, try uploading the PDF instead.
</Callout>

## Search Online

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Search Online" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **Search Online**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Pick what to search by (**Title**, **Author**, **Journal**, **Publisher** or **Keyword**), type your search, and click **Search**.

    To narrow the results, use the filters: **Source**, **Year**, **Journal**, **SJR Quartile** and **Access** (Open Access or Subscription).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add papers" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Paper** on a result to add it. To add several at once, select them and click **Add Selected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## BibTeX / RIS

Use this to bring in references from EndNote, a LaTeX project or any other tool that exports BibTeX or RIS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose BibTeX / RIS" icon="file-code" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **BibTeX / RIS**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload or paste" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Drop your `.bib` or `.ris` files into the window, or paste the citation text into the box below it. Paste either BibTeX or RIS, not both at once.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click the import button (for example **Import 1 File** or **Import 12 References**). Every reference is added at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  BibTeX and RIS files hold reference details only, not PDFs. To add the PDFs afterwards, see [Get PDFs with the Chrome Extension](/reference-manager/get-pdfs-with-extension).
</Callout>

## Import from Zotero or Mendeley

Connect your account once, then pick the collections, folders or papers you want. References come across with their PDFs, and your collections or folders become folders in Paperguide.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Import from Zotero & Mendeley](/reference-manager/import-zotero-mendeley).

## Add Manually

Use **Add Manually** for sources that have no online record, such as internal reports or unpublished work. Choose the type of source, enter its details (a title is required), and click **Save**.

## Track your imports

Imports run in the background, so you can keep working. A progress card in the corner shows **Importing Papers** while the import runs and **Import Completed** when it's done.

- Click **Show results** to see what happened to each paper, for example "Already in your library" for a skipped duplicate.
- To stop an import, click the **✕** next to it, then click **Stop import**. Papers already added stay in your library.
