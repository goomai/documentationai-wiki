## Overview

Every way of adding papers starts from the **Add Papers** button in Reference Manager. It opens a window with these options:

| Option                   | Use it when you have                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Upload PDF**           | PDF files on your computer                                              |
| **URL / DOI Import**     | DOIs or links to papers                                                 |
| **Search Online**        | A title, author, journal, publisher or topic, but no link               |
| **BibTeX / RIS**         | A `.bib` or `.ris` file, or citation text, from EndNote or another tool |
| **Import from Zotero**   | A Zotero library                                                        |
| **Import from Mendeley** | A Mendeley library                                                      |
| **Add Manually**         | A source with no online record                                          |

Paperguide fills in each paper's details (title, authors, journal and so on) and creates its citation for you, so there's usually nothing to type.

## Choose where papers go

Before you import, pick the destination under **Save to workspace / folder**. Papers are added to that workspace and filed into the folder you choose.

Keep **Skip duplicates** on (it's on by default) so papers already in your library aren't added again. It isn't shown for **Add Manually**. See [Handling Duplicates](/reference-manager/duplicates).

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Zotero and Mendeley imports work a little differently. Instead of a folder, you choose the workspace under **Import into workspace**, and each collection or folder you tick becomes a folder in Paperguide. See [Import from Zotero & Mendeley](/reference-manager/import-zotero-mendeley).
</Callout>

## Read tables, figures and scanned pages

When you add papers with PDFs, tick **Read tables, figures and scanned pages** at the bottom of the window. Paperguide then reads the tables, charts and figures in each PDF, and scanned pages too, not just the text, so AI features can answer from them and extract data from them.

The option is available on the Pro and Evidence plans and is marked **Pro** on other plans. It isn't shown for **BibTeX / RIS** or **Add Manually**, which add citation details only.

## Upload PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Upload PDF" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **Upload PDF**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your files" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Drag your PDFs into the window, or click **browse files** to pick them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click the upload button, which shows how many files you've added (for example **Upload 3 PDFs**). Paperguide reads each file and fills in the title, authors and journal. Each PDF becomes a reference with the file attached.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Up to 100 PDFs per upload. Each PDF can be up to 100 MB and 500 pages.
</Callout>

## URL / DOI Import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose URL / DOI Import" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **URL / DOI Import**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste your DOIs or links" icon="clipboard" title-type="p">
    Paste a DOI (for example `10.1038/s41586-020-2649-2`) or a link to a paper. To add several papers, paste one DOI or link per line.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click **Import Reference** (or **Import 3 References** for a list). Paperguide looks up each paper's details. When an open-access PDF is available, it's attached too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  DOIs and links that go straight to the paper's page on a journal, repository or university archive give the most complete details. If Paperguide can't find details for a link, try uploading the PDF instead.
</Callout>

## Search Online

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Search Online" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **Search Online**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Choose what to search by (**Title**, **Author**, **Journal**, **Publisher** or **Keyword**), type your query and click **Search**. You can also paste a DOI to find that exact paper. Use **Filter** to narrow the results by source, year, journal, SJR quartile or access.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select and add papers" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Click the results you want to select them. Then click **Add Selected** at the bottom of the window. It shows how many papers you've picked, for example **Add 3 Selected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## BibTeX / RIS

Use this to bring in references from EndNote, a LaTeX project or any other tool that exports BibTeX or RIS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose BibTeX / RIS" icon="file-code" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Papers** and choose **BibTeX / RIS**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload or paste" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Drop a `.bib` or `.ris` file into the window, or paste citation text into the box below it. You can upload one file at a time, up to 20 MB. When you paste, use either BibTeX or RIS, not both in the same box.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click **Import file**, or **Import 12 References** (for example) for pasted text. The import runs in the background, and a progress card shows how it's going.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If Paperguide says the pasted text isn't valid BibTeX or RIS, check the formatting in the tool you exported from and try again. To import several files, import them one after another.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  BibTeX and RIS files hold reference details only, not PDFs. To add the PDFs afterwards, see [Get PDFs with Extension](/chrome-extension/reference-manager).
</Callout>

## Import from Zotero or Mendeley

Connect your account once, then pick the collections, folders or papers you want. References come across with their PDFs, and your collections or folders become folders in Paperguide.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Import from Zotero & Mendeley](/reference-manager/import-zotero-mendeley).

## Add Manually

Use **Add Manually** for sources that have no online record, such as internal reports or unpublished work. Choose the type of source, enter its details (a title is required), and click **Save**. Fill in as many fields as you can, so its citation is complete.

## Track your imports

Imports run in the background, so you can keep working. A progress card in the corner shows **Importing Papers** while the import runs and **Import Completed** when it's done.

- Click **Show results** to see what happened to each paper, for example "Already in your library" for a skipped duplicate.
- To stop an import, click the **✕** next to it. For BibTeX/RIS, Zotero and Mendeley imports, confirm with **Stop import**. Papers already added stay in your library.
