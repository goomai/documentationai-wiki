## Overview

Folders and tags solve different problems:

- **Folders** group papers by project, such as a thesis chapter or a review. A paper can be in more than one folder.
- **Tags** label ideas that cut across projects, such as the type of study or whether you've read it yet.

## Folders

### Create a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + next to the workspace" icon="folder-plus" title-type="p">
    In the Reference Manager sidebar, click **+** (**New folder**) next to the workspace heading, such as **My References**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name it" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    In **Create Folder**, enter a **Folder Name**, such as `Chapter 2` or `Sleep and memory review`, and click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To create a folder inside another one, open the parent folder's **Folder options** menu and choose **Add Sub Folder**.

### Nest and rearrange folders

Drag one folder onto another to nest it. You can also use **Move Folder** from the **Folder options** menu. The same menu has **Rename** and **Delete**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1793813239380-4bwp4wf27r4-folder-options-menu.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1120" height="800" alt="The Folder options menu for a folder in the sidebar" caption="The Folder options menu: add subfolders, move, share, rename or delete a folder." />

Deleting a folder also deletes its subfolders. The papers in them aren't deleted: they move to **Unorganised**.

In a folder someone shared with you, the **Folder options** menu only has **Add Sub Folder** (with **Can Edit** access) and **Leave Folder**.

### Put papers in folders

Drag papers from the list onto a folder in the sidebar to **move** them there. Dragging takes the paper out of its other folders.

A paper can be in more than one folder without being duplicated. To add it to another folder and keep it where it is, select the paper and click **Add to Folder** in its detail panel. It then appears in both places, with the same notes and details.

To work with several papers at once, select them in the list and use the toolbar: **Add to Folders**, **Move**, **Remove** (from the current folder) or **Delete**.

## Tags

### Create a tag

In the **Tags** section of the sidebar, click **+** (**Add tag**). Enter a tag name, optionally pick an emoji, and click **Create**.

### Tag papers

- **One paper**: select the paper and click **Add Tag** in its detail panel.
- **Several papers**: select them in the list and click **Add Tag** in the toolbar to tag them all at once.

To remove a tag from a paper, click the **✕** on the tag in its detail panel.

### Find papers by tag

Click a tag in the sidebar to see every paper that has it. Click **No Tag** to see papers that haven't been tagged yet.

### Rename or delete tags

Click **Manage tags** in the **Tags** section of the sidebar. From there you can rename a tag or delete it. Deleting a tag removes it from every paper that has it.

## Filter and sort

Use the toolbar above the paper list to narrow down what you see:

- **Search Papers...** finds papers by title, author or PDF file name.
- **Filter** narrows the list by **Year**, **Author**, **Paper Type**, and **Has PDF** or **Has No PDF**.
- **Sort** orders the list by **Custom**, **Recently Added**, **Earliest Added**, **Title A-Z**, **Title Z-A**, **Year (newest)** or **Year (oldest)**.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  To put papers in your own order by dragging them, set **Sort** to **Custom** first.
</Callout>

## Sharing folders

Folders that other people share with you appear under **Shared With Me** in the sidebar. To share one of your own folders, open its **Folder options** menu and choose **Share Folder**. See [Share Papers](/reference-manager/share-library-workspace).
