## Overview

Need the gist of a paper fast? An AI Snapshot pulls out the key facts of a study into a structured summary, so you can decide whether a paper is worth reading in full.

## Generate an AI Snapshot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the paper" icon="book-open" title-type="p">
    Select a paper in your library to open its detail panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the AI Snapshot tab" icon="sparkles" title-type="p">
    The detail panel has four tabs: **Details**, **Chat**, **AI Snapshot** and **Notes**. Click **AI Snapshot**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate" icon="zap" title-type="p">
    Click **Generate AI Snapshot**. After a few moments the snapshot appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once generated, the snapshot is saved with the paper, so it's there the next time you open the tab.

## What's in a snapshot

The **Study Snapshot** covers:

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | What the study set out to find |
| **Methods** | How the research was done |
| **Population** | Who or what was studied |
| **Sample size** | How many participants or samples |
| **Outcomes** | What was measured |
| **Results** | What the study found |
| **Limitations** | Weaknesses the study acknowledges |

Below it, an **AI Summary** gives a short summary of the paper.

## Plans and limits

The number of AI Snapshots you can generate depends on your plan. The free plan includes a limited number of AI Snapshot credits. If you reach your plan's limit, you'll see a message in the tab with the option to upgrade. See [AI Credits](/ai-credits).
