## Search Paperguide inside your AI assistant

Search Paperguide's database of 200M+ academic papers right inside Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other AI assistant that supports MCP. Ask a research question in your assistant, review the results, and open full paper details without switching tabs. To connect, use `https://mcp.paperguide.ai/mcp`.

## What you need

- A Paperguide account on any plan, including Free
- An AI assistant that supports MCP connections
- AI credits in your Paperguide account

<Callout kind="info">

Claude and ChatGPT require a paid plan on their side for custom connectors. You do not need to install anything or manage API keys or config files.

</Callout>

## How it works

### Search papers

Ask your assistant a question such as "What are the latest RCTs on sleep and depression since 2020?" and it searches Paperguide for matching papers. Your assistant can return relevant results with key details so you can quickly decide what to open next. Each search costs 10 credits.

### Get full details

When a paper looks relevant, your assistant can fetch the full paper details from Paperguide. That includes the abstract, authors, journal information, DOI, and citation details so you can review the paper without leaving the conversation. Each fetch costs 2 credits.
