## Paperguide inside your AI assistant

Connect Paperguide to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other assistant that supports MCP, and it can search for papers and work with your reference manager while you chat. You do not need to switch tabs or learn new commands. Ask in plain language, and your assistant uses Paperguide to answer.

To connect, add this URL to your assistant:

```text
https://mcp.paperguide.ai/mcp
```

See [Connect & Manage](/mcp/connect-and-manage) for step-by-step setup.

## What you can do

### Search all papers

- **Find papers on any topic.** Your assistant searches Paperguide's database of 200M+ papers and returns matching papers with title, authors, journal, year, abstract, and DOI.
- **Look up one paper.** Give your assistant a DOI or PubMed ID and it fetches the full details, including a ready-to-use BibTeX entry.

### Work with your reference manager

- **Find papers you have saved.** Ask for a paper by title or DOI and your assistant finds it in your library.
- **Browse your library.** List your folders, or page through the papers in a folder.
- **Search inside your PDFs.** Ask a question and your assistant finds the relevant passages in the PDFs attached to your references, with page numbers.
- **Read a paper.** Your assistant can read the full text of an attached PDF to summarise it, review it, or write a report.
- **Organise your library.** Ask your assistant to create folders, rename them, and move papers into them.
- **Check your usage.** Ask how many AI credits you have left this month.

See [Use your reference manager](/mcp/reference-manager) for example prompts.

## What you need

- A Paperguide account on any plan, including Free
- An AI assistant that supports MCP connections
- AI credits in your Paperguide account

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  There is nothing to install and no API key to manage. Connect your assistant and sign in to Paperguide. Claude and ChatGPT require a paid plan on their side for custom connectors.
</Callout>

## Credits

MCP uses the same AI credits as the rest of Paperguide.

| Action                                  | Credits     |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Search all papers                       | 10          |
| Fetch one paper's full details          | 2           |
| Search inside your PDFs                 | 10          |
| Read a paper's full text                | 10 per part |
| Find, list, and browse saved references | Free        |
| Create, rename, and organise folders    | Free        |
| Check your usage                        | Free        |

Long papers are read in parts. A typical paper is 2 to 4 parts.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Failed calls are not charged. If a search returns nothing or a paper cannot be found, you keep your credits.
</Callout>
