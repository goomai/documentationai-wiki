## Search Paperguide inside your AI assistant

You can now search Paperguide's database of 200M+ academic papers right inside Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other AI assistant that supports MCP. No switching tabs, no copy-pasting — just ask your assistant a research question and get cited results from Paperguide. To get started, connect your assistant to `https://mcp.paperguide.ai/mcp`.

## What you need

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  - A Paperguide account (any plan, including Free)
  - An AI assistant that supports MCP connections
  - AI credits on your Paperguide account

  Claude and ChatGPT require a paid plan on their side for custom connectors. No installation, no API keys, no config files. You'll sign in to Paperguide through your browser when you connect.
</Callout>

## How it works

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Search papers" icon="search" horizontal="false" target="_self">
    Ask your assistant a question like "What are the latest RCTs on sleep and depression since 2020?" and it searches Paperguide for you. You can narrow results by year, citation count, open access, journal quality, and more — just describe what you want in plain language. You get up to 20 results per search with titles, authors, summaries, and links back to Paperguide. Costs 10 credits per search.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Get full details" icon="book-open" horizontal="false" target="_self">
    When you find a paper that looks relevant, your assistant can pull up the complete details — full abstract, author list, journal info, DOI, and a ready-to-use BibTeX citation. This is perfect for when you've found a few promising papers from a search and want the full picture. Costs 2 credits per fetch.
  </Card>
</Columns>
