## What costs credits

MCP uses the same AI credits as the rest of Paperguide. There is no separate allowance.

| Action                                  | Credits     |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Search all papers                       | 10          |
| Look up one paper's full details        | 2           |
| Search inside your PDFs                 | 10          |
| Read a paper's full text                | 10 per part |
| Add papers and attach PDFs              | Free        |
| Find, list, and browse saved references | Free        |
| Create, rename, and organise folders    | Free        |
| Check your usage                        | Free        |

Long papers are read in parts. A typical paper is 2 to 4 parts.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Failed calls are not charged. If a search returns nothing or a paper cannot be found, you keep your credits.
</Callout>

## Monthly credits by plan

| Plan       | Monthly credits |
| ---------- | --------------- |
| Free       | 2,000           |
| Plus       | 12,500          |
| Pro        | 50,000          |
| Max        | 150,000         |
| Enterprise | Custom          |

Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle: on your subscription anniversary for paid plans, or at the start of each calendar month on Free.

## Organization billing

If you belong to an organization, you choose which account pays when you connect each assistant: your personal plan or the organization's shared credits.

- **Per assistant.** One assistant can bill the organization while another bills your personal plan.
- **Fixed until you reconnect.** To switch, disconnect the assistant at **Settings → MCP** and connect it again.
- **Leaving the organization ends that connection.** Reconnect the assistant to bill your personal plan.

If you are not in an organization, your personal credits are used.

## Running out of credits

When the chosen account has no credits left, your assistant sees a message with the reset date. The connection stays active and works again when credits reset or you upgrade. Anything free, such as browsing your library, keeps working.

Check usage at **Settings → MCP**, or ask your assistant. For how credits work across all of Paperguide, see [AI Credits](/ai-credits).
