## Share a document

Click **Share**, enter an email, choose **Can View** or **Can Edit**, and click **Invite**. The person gets an email and a notification, and the document appears in their sidebar under **Shared with me**.

To share a whole folder, open its menu in the sidebar and choose **Share**.

### One editor at a time

Only one person can edit a document at a time. Others see **Being edited by (name) — view only** and can still read and reply to comments. When the document is free, they see **This document is now available to edit** and click **Refresh to edit**. If the editor's tab closes, the document frees up within about a minute.

## Comments

Select text and click the comment icon in the menu that appears. Click **Comments** in the top bar to see all threads, filter by **Open** or **Resolved**, reply, and **Resolve** or **Reopen** a thread.

## Versions

Go to **More → Versions**:

- **Save version**: save the current state with a name, such as "Before reviewer revisions".
- **See history**: open earlier versions. Pick one to preview, then click **Restore** or **Back to current**.

Versions are also saved automatically while you work, and before and after every AI Chat edit. Restoring first saves your current document as a new version, so nothing is lost.

## Organize documents

In the sidebar, **+ New Document** creates a document or imports a Word file. Drag documents and folders to reorder them or move them to another workspace. A folder's menu has **New Document**, **Add Sub Folder**, **Rename**, **Share** and **Delete**.

Deleting a folder also deletes the documents in it. This can't be undone.

## Export

Go to **More → Export** and choose **Word (.docx)**, **PDF (.pdf)** or **LaTeX (.zip)**. Exports include your citations and the References list. Exporting needs a paid plan.
