Describe what you want to write and AI Writer searches the literature, picks the most relevant papers, and writes a draft or an outline with citations. You can also start from a blank page or a Word file.

## Generate a document or outline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how to start" title-type="p">
    Go to **AI Writer** and pick an option:

    - **Generate Document**: AI writes a full draft with citations.
    - **Generate Outline**: AI writes the headings first, so you can shape the structure before the draft is written.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe your document" title-type="p">
    In **Describe your document**, write your topic or research question and any instructions: scope, sections, tone, and the kind of sources you want. See [Write a good description](#write-a-good-description).

    To give AI your own material, click the paperclip and attach PDFs, such as drafts, notes or papers. AI uses them as context and can cite them. Then click **Continue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your citation preferences" title-type="p">
    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1795671576463-ru1fgppwjgq-02-citation-preferences.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1456" height="827" alt="Citation preferences with Public Research Papers, Your Reference Manager, Citation Style, Number of References, Language and Word Count" />

    **Citation Sources:** turn on one or both.

    - **Public Research Papers**: 200M+ indexed papers. Click **Filters** to narrow by publication year, citations, open access, PDF availability, journal quartile, SJR, SNIP, language, paper type or field of study.
    - **Your Reference Manager**: papers from your library. Click **Choose folders** to use only some folders or papers (up to 100), and tick **Always include selected references in the document** if every one must be cited.

    If both are off, the document has no citations.

    **Other Settings:**

    - **Citation Style**: search for any style, such as APA 7 or Vancouver.
    - **Number of References**: **Standard (20 Papers)** or **Comprehensive (50 Papers)**. Generate Document only.
    - **Language**: the language the document is written in.
    - **Word Count**: a target from 200 to 20,000 words. The default is 3,000, which is also the free plan limit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate" title-type="p">
    Click **Generate Document** or **Generate Outline**. The document opens with AI Chat beside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## While it writes

The editor shows what AI Writer is doing, and the chat lists each step: searching for papers, ranking them, then writing section by section. A draft usually takes a few minutes.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-32657b98-c10c-411d-8b88-f43a8010b91d/doc-f333a884-cde9-44eb-8107-3714d598a727/1795671580046-j6n8uf50elh-03-generating.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1456" height="827" alt="A document being generated, with the paper searches listed in AI Chat" />

To stop, click the stop button in the chat box. Sections that are already written are kept.

If generation stops on its own, a notice in the editor tells you why and what to do:

| Notice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Not enough credits to generate this document | Click **Upgrade plan** or **Add credits**. Your request is saved. |
| No papers found for this request | Rephrase the description or relax the filters, then generate again. |
| We couldn't read an attached file | Attach it again as a PDF with selectable text, not a scan. |
| Connection lost while generating | Reload the page. The document may still be generating. |

## Review an outline

With **Generate Outline**, the outline goes into the editor as headings. Edit, add or delete headings right in the editor, then choose in the **Outline ready in the editor** card in the chat:

- **Generate full document**: AI writes the draft under your headings. Add a note in the box first if you want something changed or added.
- **Regenerate**: get a new outline.
- **Confirm outline**: keep the headings and write the document yourself.

## Start blank or from Word

- **Open blank document**: an empty document. Every AI tool works here too.
- **Import Word Document**: drop a .docx file. The first heading becomes the document title.

Both are on the start page. In the sidebar, **+ New Document** also offers **Create New Document** and **Import Word Document**.

## Change settings later

Go to **More → Settings** to change the **Document Prompt**, **Citation Sources** (with filters and folders), **Citation Style**, **Language** and **Font Style** (Sans or Serif). The prompt and sources are used the next time AI writes in this document.

## Write a good description

The description decides both the draft and the papers it cites. A strong description does four things:

1. **Scopes the topic.** "The effect of intermittent fasting on insulin sensitivity in adults with type 2 diabetes" works far better than "intermittent fasting".
2. **Says which sources to use.** For example, a publication window, review articles or primary research. This works together with the filters.
3. **Lists the sections you want,** such as introduction, mechanisms, clinical evidence, research gaps and conclusion.
4. **Sets the tone and audience,** such as formal academic, or concise and technical.

**Examples**

> Literature review on the role of gut microbiota in type 2 diabetes. Use studies from the last eight years and prefer high-quality journals. Sections: introduction, proposed mechanisms, clinical evidence, research gaps, conclusion. Formal academic tone.

> Background section on transformer models for time-series forecasting. Cite primary methods papers, keep it concise and technical, and go from foundational models to recent variants.

The draft is a researched starting point. Check the citations and refine it with [AI Chat and the writing tools](/ai-writer/ai-chat-and-writing-tools).
