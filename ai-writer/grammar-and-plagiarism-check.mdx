---
title: Grammar and plagiarism check
description: Check grammar and compare your document with online sources before you submit.
---
Both checks are in the **Review** menu at the top of a document.

## Grammar Checker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the check" title-type="p">
    Click **Review → Grammar Checker**, then **Check grammar**. The whole document is scanned.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fix issues" title-type="p">
    Each issue is listed in the panel and underlined in the text. Click **Accept** to apply the fix, or pick one of the **More suggestions**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check again" title-type="p">
    After editing, click **Re-check**. **Clear** removes the underlines.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The check works in supported languages. If it says your language isn't supported, change it in **More → Settings → Language**.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Gene names, abbreviations and technical terms are often flagged as spelling mistakes. Accept only real errors.
</Callout>

## Plagiarism Checker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the check" title-type="p">
    Click **Review → Plagiarism Checker**, then **Check Plagiarism**. The results open on their own page. Long documents can take up to 5 minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the score" title-type="p">
    Every sentence is compared with online sources. The **Plagiarism score** shows how much of your text matches, with a verdict from **Minimal similarity** to **High similarity**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review matches" title-type="p">
    Each match is marked **Exact match** or with a similarity percentage. Click **Show in text** to find the sentence, or open the source to compare.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Limits

| Plan | Words per check | Words per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2,000 | 4,000 |
| Plus | 5,000 | 20,000 |
| Pro | 5,000 | 100,000 |
| Evidence | 5,000 | 100,000 |

If a document is longer than your per-check limit, only the first part is checked.
