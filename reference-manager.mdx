Reference Manager is where your papers live in Paperguide, and where your research gets done.

- **Bring your papers in.** Import your library from Zotero or Mendeley in a few clicks, or add papers from a PDF, a DOI or a quick search. Paperguide fills in the details and can find the open-access PDF.
- **Keep it organized.** Group papers into folders and label them with tags.
- **Read and take notes.** Highlight and annotate PDFs. Your notes stay with the paper.
- **Get the gist fast.** An AI Snapshot pulls out a paper's methods, sample and results.
- **Work together.** Share a folder and choose who can view, add notes or edit.
- **Put it to work.** Use your saved papers in Research Agent, Literature Review, Systematic Review, Extract Data and AI Writer.
