## The dashboard

Click **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar to open your home page. It greets you with **What would you like to research today?** and a single input box where most work starts.

From here you can:

- Ask a question or start an agent task
- Pick up recent work under **Continue where you left off**
- Jump into your Reference Manager, Systematic Review, Extract Data, and AI Writer

## Agent tabs

The tabs along the top of the input box choose which agent runs your request.

| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Research Agent** | Ask your all-in-one research assistant: find papers, answer questions, compare studies, chart results, or draft text. |
| **Literature Review** | Find and synthesize papers into a review. |
| **Deep Research Report** | Generate a detailed, cited report from papers. |
| **Search** | Search 200M+ papers and get cited answers. Switch between **AI Search** and **Keyword** at the bottom of the box. |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you open **Chat with PDF** from **More tools**, it takes the fourth tab's place so the tab for the page you're on stays selected.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a tab" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Choose the agent that fits your task. **Research Agent** is selected by default.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type your question or topic" icon="edit-3" title-type="p">
    For example, "Does intermittent fasting improve insulin sensitivity in adults with type 2 diabetes?"

    On the **Research Agent** tab, click **+** to **Add or upload files** or **Add from reference manager**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set sources and filters" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Use the sources button (it shows **All sources** by default) and **Filters** to control where the agent looks. See below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    Click the orange arrow or press Enter. Your chat opens and is saved to **History**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sources

Click the sources button in the input box to open **Sources**, then switch each one on or off.

| Source | What it searches |
| --- | --- |
| **Research papers** | 200M+ public research papers |
| **PubMed** | 39M+ biomedical and life-science papers |
| **arXiv** | Research preprints |
| **Reference manager** | Papers you've saved in your Reference Manager |

- Keep at least one source on.
- With **Reference manager** on, click **Choose papers or folders** to limit the search to specific papers or folders (Research Agent and Literature Review).
- **Deep Research Report** offers only **Research papers** and **Reference manager**.

## Filters

Click **Filters** to narrow the papers searched. Filters apply when you're searching research papers, not only your Reference Manager.

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Open Access** | Free to read, no paywall |
| **Has PDF** | Full text available |
| **Publication year** | All, Last 2 years, Last 5 years, or Custom range |
| **Min citations** | Minimum number of citations |
| **Journal quality** | **Journal quartile** (Q1 only up to Q1–Q4), **SNIP score**, **SJR score** |
| **Publication details** | **Language** and **Paper type** (Article, Review, Preprint, Conference paper, Book, Dissertation, Report, Other) |
| **Fields of study** | One or more fields |

Click **Apply filters** to use them, or **Reset filters** to clear them. The number on the **Filters** button shows how many are active.

## Starter tasks

On the **Research Agent** tab, starter chips under the input fill the box with a ready-made prompt: **Search papers**, **Research gap analysis**, **Literature review**, and **Write a draft**. Replace the bracketed parts with your own topic, then send.

Click **See more examples** to open the full task grid, **What do you want the agent to do?** The same grid appears under the input when you click **New Chat**. Filter it by category:

| Category | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Search papers, Research gap analysis, Find related papers |
| **Analyze** | Answer a question, Chat with papers |
| **Review** | Screen studies, Extract data, Literature review |
| **Write** | Write a draft, Add citations |
| **Visualize** | Generate a diagram, Chart study results, Create a PRISMA diagram |

The other tabs show their own sample questions instead.

## Home sections

Below the input, the dashboard shows what's relevant to your account right now.

- **Your active review:** your most recent systematic review, its current stage, and a **Continue** button. Shown when you have a review in progress.
- **Your research workspace:** cards for **Reference Manager**, **Systematic Review**, **Extract Data**, and **AI Writer**. Depending on your account, the top card helps you:
  - set up your library (**Import from Zotero**, **Import from Mendeley**, **Import by DOI or file**),
  - start a **Systematic Review** (**Dual Review** or **AI Review**), or
  - use your library (**Ask Research agent**, **Create literature review**, **Extract data from papers**).
- **Continue where you left off:** your latest chats, reports, documents, tables, and reviews. Click one to reopen it.

## Left sidebar

The sidebar is the same everywhere in Paperguide, in both your personal account and organizations. Click the panel icon at the top to collapse it to icons.

### Workspace

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Reference Manager** | Store, organize, and read your papers. Import from Zotero, Mendeley, or files. |
| **Systematic Review** | Run systematic reviews with screening, extraction, and reporting. |
| **AI Writer** | Write documents with AI help and citations from 200M+ papers or your library. |
| **Extract Data** | Pull structured data from papers into tables. |
| **Citation Graph** | Explore how papers cite and connect to each other. |
| **My Notebooks** | Your Writer documents and data tables in one place. |

**My Notebooks** lists your **Document** and **Table** items across your workspaces, grouped by date, including items shared with you. It replaces the older **My Workbooks** page. Open an item to keep working, or select items to delete them.

### AI Agents

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **New Chat** | Start a new agent chat with the agent tabs and the full task grid. |
| **History** | Your past chats, agent runs, and shared work. Search by name, then open, rename, or delete a chat. |
| **More tools** | **Literature Review**, **Deep Research Report**, **Chat with PDF**, **AI Search**, and **Free Tools**. |

### Footer

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits meter** | Shows the AI credits you've used on your personal plan. Hover or click it for details and options to add credits. Not shown in organizations. |
| **Get Extension** | Install the Paperguide browser extension. Hidden once it's installed. |
| **Connect MCP** | Connect Paperguide to an AI assistant (opens **Settings → MCP**). |
| **Help & Demo** | **Support chat**, **Join Slack community**, **Help center**, **Help videos**, demo calls, and the **Changelog**. |

### Account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to:

- Switch between your **Personal** account and any organizations you belong to
- Open **Settings**
- Change the **System language**
- **Join Slack**
- **Logout**

If you're on the free plan, an **Upgrade** button appears below your name.
