## Overview

The Paperguide Chrome extension connects your browser to Paperguide. It does four jobs:

- **Saves papers** from any site to your Reference Manager, with their PDF and citation details.
- **Fills in missing PDFs** for papers in your Reference Manager and in a systematic review's full text screening, one at a time or in bulk.
- **Uses your library access** to get PDFs behind publisher paywalls.
- **Copies citations and answers questions** about the page you're reading.

## Paperguide features that need the extension

These options in Paperguide run through the extension. In their menus, they're marked with an extension badge:

- **Find PDF online**: finds and attaches PDFs for one paper or many.
- **Locate PDF**: opens a paper's page and attaches the PDF when you open it.
- **Search Google for PDF**: searches Google for a paper's PDF in a new tab.

If you use one of them without the extension, Paperguide asks you to install it first.

## Troubleshooting

<Expandable title="Paperguide says the extension isn't installed" default-open="false">
  Check that the extension is installed and that you're logged in to it with the same Paperguide account you use in the app. Then refresh the Paperguide tab.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="“Update the Paperguide extension to find PDFs for many papers at once”" default-open="false">
  Your extension is out of date. Update it from Chrome's extensions page, then try **Find PDF online** again.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="“A PDF search is already running in another Paperguide tab”" default-open="false">
  Only one PDF search can run at a time. Wait for the other search to finish, or close the tab it's running in.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="The PDF search stopped" default-open="false">
  The search runs in your browser, so it stops if you close Chrome. Open the **Find PDFs** dialog again and click **Fetch all** to fetch the rest.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Most papers end up behind a paywall" default-open="false">
  Paperguide can only get open-access PDFs on its own. If your university or library has access, [set up library access](/chrome-extension/library-access) and fetch again.
</Expandable>
