---
title: Choose AI Content Language
---
Paperguide can write AI answers, reports, documents, and extracted data in many languages. Set a default once, then change it for individual Writer documents, reports, or tables when you need to.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This page covers the language of AI-generated content. To change the language of Paperguide's menus and buttons, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **System language**.
</Callout>

## Set your default AI Generation Language

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**.
2. Open **General**.
3. Under **AI Generation Language**, pick your language from the dropdown.

Agent chats (Research Agent, Literature Review, Chat with PDF, and Search) answer in this language. There's no separate language control inside a chat. If your default is English (US), the agent replies in the language you write your question in.

### Supported languages

- English (US and UK)
- German
- French
- Spanish
- Portuguese (EU and BR)
- Italian
- Japanese
- Chinese (Mandarin)
- Indonesian
- Thai
- Vietnamese
- Others

<Callout uid="cbe27851-e4c2-4dfe-a811-99b729f3d609" kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  **Language not listed?** Ask for it in your prompt, for example: *Explain (your question) in Russian.* This works in agent chats and in Writer.
</Callout>

## Change the language for one item

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Writer document** | In the document, click **More → Settings**, then choose a **Language**. AI writing and grammar checks use this language for that document. |
| **Deep Research Report** | After Paperguide confirms your research question, pick a language from the dropdown next to the start buttons, then start the report. |
| **Extract Data table** | Click **Manage Columns**, open the **Settings** tab, and choose an **Output language**. Extracted values and explanations are written in this language. |

<Callout uid="7a68e789-549f-4264-b0ff-95532ac0cd83" kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If your writing language isn't available in Writer, choose "Others." AI writes in English, and you can ask for another language in a command, such as "Write an introduction in Polish."
</Callout>
