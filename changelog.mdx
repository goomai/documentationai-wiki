  ## Paperguide MCP Server

  You can now search Paperguide's database of 200M+ academic papers directly inside your AI assistant. Connect Paperguide to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Codex, or any other client that supports MCP — no API keys, no config files, no manual setup.

  ### What you can do

  - **Search papers:** Ask your assistant a research question and it searches Paperguide for matching papers with key details.
  - **Get full details:** When a paper looks relevant, your assistant fetches the full abstract, authors, journal info, DOI, and citation details — all without leaving the conversation.

  ### How it works

  Add `https://mcp.paperguide.ai/mcp` as a remote server in your assistant. Sign in with your Paperguide account and you're connected. MCP uses your existing AI credit balance — 10 credits per search, 2 per paper fetch. Failed calls are not charged.

  If you belong to an organization, you can choose which credit account pays for MCP usage when you connect each app.

  [Learn more](/mcp/overview)

<Update label="20 July, 2026" tags={["new feature"]}>
  ## Systematic Review

  You can now run a full systematic review inside Paperguide — from research question to a synthesized report with a PRISMA diagram — with AI doing the work at every stage and a person verifying each decision before the review moves forward.

  ### What's included

  - **Protocol:** Define eligibility criteria and extraction fields. AI generates a starting set from your research question, which you edit.
  - **Papers:** Collect papers from AI Search queries or your reference manager into a single set, with duplicate detection.
  - **Title & Abstract Screening:** AI screens every abstract against your criteria, gives a decision and exclusion reason, and cites the evidence.
  - **Full Text Screening:** AI screens full-text PDFs with a per-criterion summary and evidence.
  - **Data Extraction:** AI extracts your defined fields into a table, with citations back to the source statement.
  - **Report:** AI synthesizes the extracted data into a report with full methodology and an auto-generated PRISMA diagram.

  ### Why it matters

  Every screening decision records both what AI proposed and what a human changed. Every extracted value links to the statement it came from. The PRISMA diagram updates automatically as decisions change. That audit trail is what makes the output defensible.

  You can run a review solo or add a team with role-based verification — assign verifiers to stages so responsibilities are clear from the start.

  [Learn more](/systematic-review/overview)
</Update>

<Update label="17 July, 2026" tags={["new feature"]}>
  ## Team workspaces are live

  A workspace is a shared library your whole team works in. Everything created inside it is available to everyone in it. No forwarding PDFs, no exporting reference lists, no asking who has the latest version.

  In a workspace, your team can:

  - Build and annotate a shared reference library
  - Write and collaborate on documents
  - Extract data from papers together
  - Run systematic reviews as a team

  You can create separate workspaces for different projects, labs, or groups, and control who has access to each one. An admin dashboard shows usage and access in one place.

  ### How workspaces work

  Paperguide uses an organization and workspace hierarchy. Your organization is the top-level team container, and each workspace holds its own Reference Manager, AI Writer, Systematic Review, Extract Data, and Notebooks.

  There are three workspace types:

  - **Personal** — your sovereign library, created automatically when you sign up. Not part of any organization.
  - **Private** — your personal space inside an organization. Only you can see it, and even organization admins cannot access it.
  - **Workspaces** — a shared collaborative space inside an organization. Add multiple team members with different roles, and create as many team workspaces as your team needs.

  ### Roles & access control

  Organization roles (Owner, Admin, Member) control who can manage billing, seats, and workspaces. Workspace control what someone can do inside a specific team workspace — so you can add someone to the organization without giving them access to every shared workspace.

  ### Getting started

  Upgrade your plan with 2 or more seats to create an organization account and collaborative workspaces. Then invite team members by email, pre-assign them to specific workspaces, and Paperguide handles the rest.

  [Upgrade your plan](https://paperguide.ai/pricing/) / [Learn more](/organizations-workspaces)
</Update>

<Update label="25 May, 2026" tags={["improvement"]}>
  ## Search results got a major upgrade

  We rebuilt the search pipeline behind Paperguide. The difference is noticeable.

  **Before:** A small pool of candidate papers, ranked by relevance alone.

  **Now:** Every search evaluates 10,000+ candidates through a multi-stage pipeline:

  - **Wider retrieval:** Thousands of candidates pulled from both semantic and keyword-based search.
  - **Quality scoring:** Each candidate scored on citation velocity, journal impact (SNIP), and recency, not just relevance.
  - **Reranking:** Relevance scores blended with quality signals to surface papers that are both on-topic and published in credible journals.

  Better results, stronger sources, across everything: AI Search, Literature Review, Research Agent, AI Writer.

  You don't need to change anything. It's already live.
</Update>

  ## Research Agent improvements

  We've been testing the Research Agent hard over the past few weeks. Running evaluations on response quality, tracking where it breaks, and reading every piece of feedback you sent in.

  This week we shipped a retrained model on top of upgraded base AI models. Here's what changed:

  - **Upgraded AI models:** The Research Agent now runs on stronger underlying models, which means better reasoning, more coherent outputs, and more reliable citations across every task.
  - **Smarter task understanding:** The agent is better at interpreting what you're actually asking, whether it's a lit review, a comparison, a gap analysis, or a data extraction task.
  - **More accurate responses:** We fixed a set of hallucination issues where the agent would generate claims not grounded in the source papers. Outputs are now more tightly tied to the literature.
  - **Better research task handling:** Multi-step workflows like screening, extracting, and synthesizing run more reliably from start to finish.
