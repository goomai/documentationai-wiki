## **How to Refer a Friend?**

<Steps uid="dbfb72f3-b2b5-4a87-9816-6a650bfaa3d8">
  <Step title="Open the referrals page" title-type="p">
    Log in to Paperguide, then go to [paperguide.ai/referrals](https://paperguide.ai/referrals/). You can also click **Referrals** in the Company section of the paperguide.ai website footer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy your referral link" title-type="p">
    Under **Share direct link with your peers**, click **copy**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share it" title-type="p">
    Send the link to friends, or click **Share on X** or **Share on Facebook**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To invite someone by email instead, enter their address under **Send invite via email address** and click **Send Invite**.

To see your level, referral history, and earnings, click **View my referrals and earnings** on the same page.

## Earning Rewards, Levels & Conditions

### **Rewards and Levels for Successful Referrals**

Earn rewards based on the number of **successful referrals** as follows:

- **Level 1:** $15 reward for each of the **first 5 referrals**.
- **Level 2:** $20 reward for each referral from the **6th to the 10th referral**.
- **Level 3:** $25 reward for each referral starting from the **11th referral onward**.

### Reward Conditions

- Rewards are given only if the referred friend **registers using the referral link** and **subscribes to a Paperguide plan within 30 days**.
- **Payout Timing:** Rewards are paid on a **net-30 basis** (30 days after the referred friend subscribes to a paid plan).
- **Cookie Validity:** Referral cookies remain valid for **30 days** from the time the referral link is clicked.

## Bonuses for Referred Friends

Discounts for **new users** on Paperguide **subscription** plans.

### Referred friends enjoy the following discounts:

- **30% discount** on annual subscription plans.
- **20% discount** on monthly subscription plans.

<Callout uid="8534966c-0b84-47ea-ac23-5a4489497b51" kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Your friend gets the discount by signing up through your referral link. It's applied automatically at checkout.
</Callout>

### Discount Conditions

- Discounts are applied **only** when the friend registers through the referral link.
- If a referred friend uses multiple referral links, the reward is credited to the **owner of the last referral link used**.
