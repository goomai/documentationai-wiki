## What is Deep Research Report?

Deep Research Report finds and screens papers on your topic, gathers their key insights, and writes a detailed report with citations. Use it when you want a thorough written overview of a topic, not just a quick answer.

## Start a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Deep Research Report" icon="file-search" title-type="p">
    On your Dashboard or in **New Chat**, select the **Deep Research Report** tab. You can also open **More tools** → **Deep Research Report** in the sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe your topic" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Type your topic, research question, and any requirements. For example: "How are GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic changing obesity treatment?"

    By default, the report uses public **Research papers**. To build it from your own library instead, open the sources button, turn on **Reference manager**, and turn off **Research papers**. When you use research papers, you can also click **Filters** to narrow the papers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the scope" icon="sliders-horizontal" title-type="p">
    Send your question. In **Select your literature review scope**, choose how many papers to scan and summarize:

    - **Quick**: the default, and the fastest.
    - **Standard**: a larger set of papers.
    - **Comprehensive**: the largest set, for the most in-depth report.

    On the free plan, **Standard** and **Comprehensive** are marked **Upgrade**. Click **Generate report**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refine your question" icon="message-circle" title-type="p">
    Paperguide may ask a few questions to sharpen your research question. Reply in the chat. When the question is ready, choose the report's language, then pick how to run it:

    - **Automate Deep Research**: Paperguide runs every stage for you and writes the report.
    - **Controlled Deep Research**: you review each stage and click **Continue** to move on. You can add or remove papers before they're screened.
  </Step>
</Steps>

##

## Find your reports later

Every report is saved automatically. Open **History** in the sidebar and filter by **Deep Research** to find it.
